# Second Brain

The working instructions for Nathan Baron's second brain. The knowledge itself lives in Notion; this repository holds only the instructions that act on it: skills, writing standards and the prompts that scheduled routines run. Nothing personal, and nothing from his notes, is ever copied into this repository.

## The frame: CODE

- **Capture.** TwinMind captures stretches of his day. He adds notes by hand in Notion.
- **Organize.** The `intake` skill turns captured conversations into notes and To Process tasks, routed through Areas. Tags are retired; Areas, people, vessels and links between notes carry the structure.
- **Distill.** Facet context pages, subject syntheses and summaries on older notes. (To come.)
- **Express.** The morning briefing, meeting prep, checking the archive before writing, and answering what we know about a subject. (To come.)

## Rules that hold everywhere

- Nothing becomes real without him. Tasks land as To Process, and he triages.
- Every word written to Notion follows `standards/writing-notes.md`.
- Facets keep their own rules, and nothing from one facet is pulled into another's output unless he asks.
- Never write to the MES Activity Log or the calendar. Flag candidates instead.
- Use Toronto time (`TZ=America/Toronto date`), 24-hour clock, Canadian spelling, vessel names in ALL CAPS.
- Never fabricate. If something can't be read or settled, say so in the report.

## Layout

- `.claude/skills/intake/`: the intake skill and `chunk.py`.
- `standards/writing-notes.md`: how notes and tasks are written.
- `prompts/`: the exact prompts the scheduled routines run.

## Changing things

Commit straight to `main` with a clear message. When a skill changes, Claude.ai's uploaded copy has to be uploaded again; say so.
